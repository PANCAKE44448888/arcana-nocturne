# Arcana Nocturne

A dark-fantasy poker duel played at the candlelit tables of the Midnight Circuit.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or dependencies are required.

Play Texas Hold'em through pre-flop, flop, turn, and river. Check, raise, or fold;
then compare the best five-card hand from your hole cards and the community board.
The custom 52-card deck features magical cat familiars matched to each suit.
Cast spells with mana, and select one of your cards before using Runeshift or
Lucky Draw. Every third victory advances your place in the tournament.
